SAVE OUR NILE

The Nile is more than a river. It is a source of life, history, and culture.

Why Does the Nile Matter?

  1. water

    The Nile provides an important source of water for millions of people.

  2. life

    The river supports plants, animals, and many different ecosystems.

  3. history

    The Nile has played an important role in Egyptian civilization for thousands of years.

But the Nile faces environmental challenges, and protecting it is everyone's responsibility.
