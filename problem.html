THE PROBLEM

The Nile faces several environmental challenges. Pollution and waste can harm the river and the life that depends on it.

Plastic Waste

Plastic bottles, bags, and other waste can end up in the river. This waste can harm aquatic life and make the river environment less healthy.

Water Pollution

Different forms of pollution can affect the quality of water in the Nile. Keeping pollutants out of the river helps protect its ecosystem.

Human Activities

Human activities around the river can create waste and pollution. Responsible behavior is important for keeping the Nile clean.

Why Should We Care?

A polluted river can affect water quality, aquatic life, and the environment around it.

The good news is that everyone can help through small and responsible actions.
